# Eval — do-ia

Run the prompt once without the skill and once with `do-ia`.

## Prompt

Build a polished desktop-first web app for a multi-location restaurant operations SaaS called **Tableline**. Owners and managers use it to run 12 restaurants.

It needs sales, COGS and labor KPIs and trends; live orders; reservations; staff schedules and time-off requests; inventory, low-stock alerts and purchase orders; suppliers; menu items and pricing; customer feedback; incidents; maintenance tickets; locations; team members and roles; billing; integrations; notifications; exports and reports; and an audit log.

Use realistic mock data and make the major interactions functional. Use Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind and shadcn/ui. Keep it clean, professional and information-dense without feeling cluttered. Do not ask follow-up questions; make reasonable assumptions and build it.

## Expected skill effect

A stronger result should:

- treat Tableline as a multi-surface product
- restrict the dashboard to overview/attention/next actions
- create coherent canonical destinations
- separate operational work from administration
- use collection/detail/workflow patterns where appropriate
- avoid giant tab sets and modal applications
- preserve meaningful drill-down and filter context
